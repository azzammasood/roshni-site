Method and sources

About the data

What this site shows

Roshni shows the load-shedding schedules that IESCO and K-Electric publish, and the planned shutdowns IESCO announces in press releases. These are plans, not measurements: a feeder listed as scheduled may have had power, and a feeder not listed may not have.

IESCO interruption levels

IESCO publishes a playbook: one schedule for each interruption level (2 to 12 hours a day). Its files never say which level is in force, so this site never assumes one. You choose the level.

Time

All times are Pakistan Standard Time (Asia/Karachi, UTC+5, no daylight saving). A window such as 22:35–02:05 crosses midnight; the end minute is not included.

Matching

Feeders are matched to census localities and press-release names by name only. A match's percentage is the name similarity after ignoring case, spacing and punctuation; 100% means identical names, which are accepted automatically. Other matches are decided by written rules, then by a language model choosing only among listed candidates, then by Claude; each shows its confidence, who accepted it and why. Unmatched names are shown as printed.

Glossary

Scheduled
A published plan for when a feeder's power is to be switched off. This site shows schedules only; it never measures whether power actually went off.
Feeder
A distribution line from a grid station that supplies a neighbourhood. Schedules are published per feeder.
IBC
K-Electric's Integrated Business Centre, the company's own service area in Karachi.
Interruption level
IESCO's scenario for how many hours a day feeders are switched off (2 to 12). IESCO publishes one schedule per level and does not say which level is in force.
