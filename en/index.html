Load shedding, as the companies report it

In FY 2024-25, GEPCO and IESCO reported no load shedding to NEPRA; the other companies reported between 0.02 and 10.25 hours a day. QESCO (Quetta) reported the most: 10.25 hours a day.

NEPRA's caveat These are the companies' own figures, not measurements. NEPRA states that some companies under-report: for FY 2024-25 its monitoring found more than 8 to 10 hours a day in PESCO, QESCO, SEPCO and HESCO.

What these numbers are Average daily load-shedding hours that each distribution company reported to NEPRA in its annual performance report, as compiled in NEPRA's Performance Evaluation Reports. One value per company and fiscal year (July to June), taken from the earliest report that lists the year. NEPRA's FY 2023-24 report link returns an error, so FY 2023-24 comes from the FY 2024-25 report's five-year table.
